Frequently asked questions

What does the course include?

The course in Spanish and English, a printed copy of the book in the language you choose (Spanish or English), the basic app downloadable for Windows and Mac computers, and a checklist to move forward step by step. The premium version is optional: it is activated from the app and includes, for now, the digital book. details to be confirmed

Do I need to have studied business administration?

No. The course is based on a book that explains its terms and includes a glossary of key words.

Does it work for a small business?

Yes. It is designed to be applied in any kind of organization.

What does the book not cover?

It does not go deeply into marketing, capital or taxes. Its focus is on organizing work, communicating, measuring and acting.

Who is behind the book?

Ravenwood Management Group, a training organization operating since 1992. The book is based on the works of L. Ron Hubbard.
